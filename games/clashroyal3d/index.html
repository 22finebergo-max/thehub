How to play

🎴 Tap a card, then tap your half of the field to place that character.

💧 Characters cost elixir. It refills over time, and fills twice as fast in the last minute.

👑 Destroy towers to earn crowns. Knock down the king tower to win straight away. After 3 minutes the side with more crowns wins.

🏆 Wins give trophies. Reach 300 for Bridge Arena and 600 for Crown Arena, and unlock new characters on the way.

Haider is the Bowler and throws Zainabs. Amaan is the Giant and only attacks towers. Josh is the Knight, Bartu the Archers, Samuel the Goblins and Joe the flying Minions.
